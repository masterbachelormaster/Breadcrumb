The Findings and Discussion section has a clear overall trajectory from quantitative patterns to qualitative mechanisms and then to the concept of individualized adoption, but several inaccurate roadmaps, scope mismatches, and misplaced passages interrupt that trajectory. Redundancy is more concentrated: the Findings sometimes narrate tables row by row, while the Discussion repeatedly restates the same access, competence, and task-level judgment mechanisms in adjacent paragraphs. The most consequential problems occur in the opening signposting, the placement of the supplementary user-only survey item and personal-data example, the framing of Section 6.2, and the unfinished notes block in Section 6.3. In tightening repetition, the interpretive links between the quantitative and qualitative evidence should be retained; the recommended cuts therefore target duplicated reporting and repeated conclusions rather than the synthesis that carries the mixed-methods argument.
